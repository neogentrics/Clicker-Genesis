# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project and there is no moderation team — there is
one person, who would rather spend the time building than refereeing.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This layout breaks on my phone" is
  useful. "This is garbage" is not.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline your suggestion, and that a decline is
  usually about scope or a stated project constraint rather than about you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or issues.

## On the subject matter

This project is built around scripture, which means people will arrive with
strongly held and differing beliefs — including none.

**Discussion of the game, its mechanics, its accuracy and its design is
welcome. Proselytising and religious argument are not**, in either direction.
This is a repository for building software. There are better places for that
conversation and this is not one of them.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue, or privately via the contact route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it is sensitive.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and block
accounts. For anything serious, the first response is usually a block rather
than a warning — a solo project does not have the capacity for a process, and
that is the honest position rather than a threat.
